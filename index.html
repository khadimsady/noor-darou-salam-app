🕌 Learn How to Pray

Step-by-step Salah lessons following the Maliki school.

  1. Understand the conditions of prayer and purification.
  2. Make the intention (Niyyah).
  3. Stand facing the Qibla.
  4. Say Allahu Akbar to begin prayer.
  5. Recite Al-Fatiha and the appropriate recitation.
  6. Perform Ruku (bowing).
  7. Stand upright again.
  8. Perform Sujud (prostration).
  9. Complete the remaining Rak'ahs.
  10. Finish with Tashahhud and Salam.

Detailed lessons, demonstrations and Maliki references will be added.

🕋 Step-by-Step Umrah Guide

  1. Prepare for Ihram and make the intention.
  2. Enter Ihram and recite the Talbiyah.
  3. Enter Al-Masjid Al-Haram.
  4. Perform seven rounds of Tawaf.
  5. Pray two Rak'ahs when appropriate.
  6. Perform Sa'i between Safa and Marwah.
  7. Complete seven circuits of Sa'i.
  8. Shave or shorten the hair to complete Umrah.

Detailed guidance and supplications will be added.
