🤲 Learn How to Pray

Maliki School of Jurisprudence

  1. Purification (Wudu).
  2. Intention (Niyyah).
  3. Takbir (Starting Prayer).
  4. Recite Al-Fatiha.
  5. Perform Ruku (bowing).
  6. Stand upright.
  7. Perform Sujud (prostration).
  8. Complete the Rak'ahs.
  9. Perform Tashahhud.
  10. Finish with Salam.

🕋 Step-by-Step Umrah Guide

  1. Preparation and Ihram.
  2. Entering Ihram and intention.
  3. Talbiyah.
  4. Tawaf around the Kaaba.
  5. Praying two Rak'ahs.
  6. Sa'i between Safa and Marwah.
  7. Halq or Taqsir.
  8. Completion of Umrah.
